2048 Support

How to play

Swipe in any direction to move all tiles. Tiles with the same number merge when they meet. Keep combining tiles to reach 2048.

Saved games

Your current board and best score are saved automatically on your device. Use the undo button to reverse the previous move or the restart button to begin a new game.

Email support@etfradarapp.com for help, feedback, or accessibility support.
